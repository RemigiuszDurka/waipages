WHAT THE FILE CAN — AND CANNOT — SAY

The universe did not change.
Our view did.

The archive records discovered planets under changing instruments, search programs and reporting. It cannot estimate the underlying frequency of planet types. The original CSV, conversions and every plotted point can be traced and reproduced.
