INTERPRETATION RULE

Counts over 1965–2016 depend on catalog coverage and changing detection practice. The file begins at M5.5. Without magnitude type, depth, exposure and a completeness model, these charts cannot estimate hazard or predict the next event.

THE QUESTION THAT REMAINS

The map shows where records are.
It cannot say where danger is next.

Patterns in a historical sample invite better questions about tectonics, catalog completeness and exposure. They do not replace a scientific seismic-hazard model.
