Two forest questions; two different datasets.

This study uses World Bank/FAO forest area as a percentage of land area. Global Forest Watch measures tree-cover loss with another definition and method. GFW specifically warns that its Madagascar loss data underreport years before 2015; a pre/post-2015 break there is not evidence of a physical jump. Read the GFW caveat ↗

WHAT THE LINES CANNOT SHOW

These are national aggregates. The island silhouette locates Madagascar; it does not encode where within the country a change occurred. None of the comparisons identifies a cause or predicts a future value.
